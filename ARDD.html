Overview

Context

This project explores the boundary between physical space and digital augmentation through the lens of Tim Burton's visual universe. Three distinct worlds — Alice in Wonderland, Beetlejuice, and The Nightmare Before Christmas — each become a filter that overlays the real world with character, atmosphere and motion.

The AR filters were designed to feel like entering a dimension rather than applying a cosmetic layer, prioritising narrative immersion over novelty.

Intent

To translate the emotional texture of Tim Burton's filmmaking — the gothic, the whimsical, the melancholic — into a spatial augmented reality experience that people can step inside.
