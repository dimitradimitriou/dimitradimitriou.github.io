Background

I'm Dimitra, a multidisciplinary designer and operations leader who turns the invisible into the experiential. With a 10-year background in F&B management, I apply systems thinking and sensory design to translate complex, intangible inputs — from service systems to neural data — into brand identities, spatial environments, and digital experiences that people can feel.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy, aesthetics, and lived experience. I believe design is not decoration — it is the architecture of how people move through the world, what they feel in a space, and what they carry long after they've left.
